Okay, here's a summary of the recurring issues:

*   **Baler Issues:** Frequent baler binding wire replacements and issues with wire routing.
*   **Voidfill Problems:** Common issues include tape head problems (blade pad missing, tape build-up), box gripping problems, paper feed jams, and misalignment of photoeyes/barcode scanners.
*   **Fork Lift Issues:**  Frequent meter readings and some electrical troubleshooting (fuses, corroded connectors).
*   **Other:**  Pole covers installation, gate misalignment, and general equipment checks.
