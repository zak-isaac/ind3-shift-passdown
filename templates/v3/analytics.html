Technician WO participation

Each participating technician receives one count per distinct accepted WO. Shared WOs can appear for multiple technicians.

What these numbers do and do not measure Analytics uses Cetaris WO numbers, asset IDs, technician labor participation, accepted RFR categories and PM completion from confirmed uploads. It does not infer equipment downtime, operating degradation, monitoring events, MTTR, or failure duration from a work-order status or a legacy priority field. Excluded work orders and manual entries are not included in these Cetaris charts.
