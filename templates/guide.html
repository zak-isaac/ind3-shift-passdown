💡 1. What is the Passdown System?

The IND3 Reliability Passdown System is a digital shift handover tool built for the Walmart IND3 facility's Reliability team. It replaces paper logs and email chains with a live, real-time system that captures every work order, asset issue, and action taken during a shift — and automatically formats it into a professional report.

Why it exists

  • Technicians work on assets all shift but information gets lost between shifts.
  • MSTs (Maintenance Shift Technicians / leads) need a clear picture of the shift — fast.
  • Reports used to be written manually, taking 15–30 minutes and often missing details.
  • This tool reduces that to under 2 minutes, with accurate data pulled directly from Cetaris.

What it does

  • Lets technicians log work orders with their name, asset, issue, action, and result.
  • Accepts Cetaris (CMMS) file exports — no re-typing what's already in the system.
  • Shows MST a live feed of all activity as it happens.
  • Auto-generates a formatted passdown report ready to send to the next shift.
  • Tracks metrics over time: most worked-on assets, WO trends, PM counts, and more.
💡 Think of it like this: Techs log what they did. MST reviews and curates. The system writes the report. Everyone goes home on time.

🔐 3. Technician Login

From the home screen, a technician enters their associate number (the numeric ID on their badge). No password needed — the system matches the number to the roster and identifies them by name.

Step by step

  1. Open the passdown URL on any device (phone, tablet, or PC) on the facility network.
  2. The home page shows a list of available shifts. Select the one that is currently active.
  3. Enter your associate number and tap Check In.
  4. The system greets you by name and loads your personal dashboard.
⚠️ If your associate number isn't recognized, ask your MST to verify your name is on the roster in the Admin panel.

What you see after login (Tech Dashboard)

  • My Shift Happenings — all entries you have logged this shift (read-only).
  • Log New Entry — a form to manually add a work order.
  • Upload from Cetaris — drag-and-drop your exported CSV file.
  • Carry-Over from Previous Shift — issues the previous shift flagged for follow-up.

📤 5. CMMS / Cetaris File Upload

Instead of manually retyping everything, you can export your work orders from Cetaris and upload the CSV directly. The system reads the file, extracts your WOs, and creates entries automatically.

How to export from Cetaris

  1. Log in to Cetaris and navigate to your Work Orders for the shift.
  2. Filter by your name and today's date.
  3. Click Export → CSV.
  4. Save the file to your device.

How to upload

  1. On your tech dashboard, scroll to Upload from Cetaris.
  2. Drag your CSV file into the upload zone, or tap to browse.
  3. The system parses the file and shows a summary: how many WOs were imported, and how many were PMs (PMs are counted but not shown as happenings).
  4. Review the entries that appeared in My Shift Happenings.

Smart duplicate handling

  • If you re-upload the same file, your existing entries are updated in place — no duplicates.
  • If a different tech worked the same WO number, both entries are kept — the system understands two people can work the same asset.
  • In the final report, duplicate WO numbers are consolidated into a single report line.
⚠️ PM (Preventive Maintenance) work orders are detected automatically and excluded from the Shift Happenings list. They are still counted and shown as a PM total in the report.

🚀 6. Starting a Shift (MST)

The MST (lead) is responsible for opening and closing shifts. Only one shift can be active at a time.

Starting a new shift

  1. From the home page, click MST / Lead Login.
  2. Enter the MST PIN (set by admin).
  3. Click Open New Shift.
  4. Select the shift type: S1 (5:30 AM–4:00 PM), S2 (12:00 PM–10:30 PM), S3, etc.
  5. Enter your name as lead. The shift is now live.

📡 7. Live Feed — What MST Sees

The MST dashboard has a Live Feed that shows all entries logged this shift, in real time. Every time a tech submits an entry, it appears here automatically without needing to refresh the page (WebSocket-powered).

What each entry card shows

  • Tech avatar — first initial of the technician's name, in blue.
  • Tech name — who logged the entry.
  • Relative time — "5 minutes ago", "just now", etc.
  • Exact time — timestamp in 24hr format.
  • Unit badge — the asset ID, monospace font.
  • Issue text — formatted as UNIT: description (WO #number).
  • Priority badge — Down, Degraded, or Monitor.

Color stripe on the left

  • Red stripe — asset is Down (out of service).
  • Yellow stripe — asset is Degraded (impaired).
  • Green stripe — asset is being Monitored (resolved).

Excluded entries

Entries excluded from the report are shown grayed out with strikethrough and a red "Excluded from report" label. They still appear in the live feed so MST can reverse the decision if needed.

Status bar

At the top of the feed: total log count, count of Down / Degraded / Monitor assets, and a live green dot confirming the WebSocket connection is active.

💡 If the live dot turns blue/pulsing, the feed is waiting for connection. Check your network connection — you must be on the facility WiFi or VPN.

🎛️ 8. MST Entry Controls

On every entry card in the live feed, MST sees four icon buttons on the right side:

ButtonIconWhat it does
Edit ✏️ Opens an inline edit form right inside the card. MST can rewrite the issue description — useful when a tech's notes are unclear or incomplete. Click Save to apply or Cancel to discard.
Exclude 🚫 Marks the entry as excluded from the report. The entry still shows in the live feed (grayed out) so it's not lost, but it will not appear in the passdown report. Click again to re-include. Use this for duplicate entries or entries a tech left notes on but aren't worth reporting.
Carry-Over 📌 Flags this asset as something the next shift should follow up on. Carry-over entries appear in a highlighted section at the top of the next shift's tech dashboard and in the "Carried Over" section of the report.
Delete 🗑️ Permanently removes the entry from the system. Requires confirmation. Use sparingly — there is no undo. Prefer Exclude if you just don't want it in the report but want to keep the record.
⚠️ Delete is permanent. If you're unsure, use Exclude instead — you can always re-include it later.

📄 9. Generating the Report

When the shift is ending, MST generates the passdown report from the MST dashboard.

Step by step

  1. Review the live feed — exclude any entries that shouldn't be in the report.
  2. Set carry-over flags on any assets the next shift should watch.
  3. Add any shift notes in the Notes field (optional — goes into the report header).
  4. Click Generate Report.
  5. The report is built instantly and shown in a preview panel.
  6. Click Copy to Clipboard to paste it into Teams, email, or wherever your facility sends passdowns.
  7. Optionally: Print Report for a formatted PDF-ready print view.

What triggers report generation

  • Manual generation (button click) — most common.
  • Auto-termination — if a shift runs 1 hour past its scheduled end, the system closes it. A report is NOT auto-generated; MST should still generate one manually.
💡 You can generate the report multiple times. Each generation saves a snapshot. The most recent one is the official record.

📋 10. Report Format Explained

The generated report follows this structure, top to bottom:

  1. Header — WALMART RELIABILITY OPERATIONS, facility, date, shift type, prepared by.
  2. Vendors — any vendor activity logged during the shift.
  3. Shift Happenings — one line per asset worked on, in this format:
PARKING0002: Yard buddy and also assisting with installing the horn and the side light. (WO #27779109).
VOIDFILL0101: First I inspected the area and noticed the taper was not seated properly. (WO #27798811).
CR0005: Unplugged the machine, waited a few minutes then plugged it back up and it's back working.
  • Asset ID — bold, before the colon.
  • Description — the tech's note, cleaned of Cetaris prefixes.
  • WO reference — appended in parentheses when a WO number is available.
  • If multiple techs worked the same WO number, they appear as one line in the report.
  1. Reliable Operations (RTS) — assets returned to service.
  2. Out of Service (OOS) — assets still down with next steps.
  3. PM Summary — count of Preventive Maintenance WOs completed.
  4. Shift Summary — AI-generated or manual summary paragraph.
💡 The report is designed to be copy-pasted directly into Teams or email. The formatting is intentional — all readers can scan it in under 60 seconds.

📌 11. Carry-Over Entries

A carry-over is an asset that was worked on this shift but needs the next shift to follow up. MST pins them with the 📌 button.

How it works

  • MST clicks 📌 on any entry in the live feed — it turns orange to confirm.
  • When the next shift starts and any tech logs in, they see a Carry-Over panel at the top of their dashboard showing exactly which assets need attention.
  • Carry-overs also appear in the generated report under a dedicated section.
  • They carry the asset ID, the original issue description, and which shift they came from.
💡 Use carry-overs liberally. It's the system's way of keeping the next shift from discovering problems blind.

🗂️ 13. Shift History

Available via MST Dashboard → History. Shows a list of all past shifts with their date, shift type, lead name, entry count, and whether a report was generated.

  • Click any shift to view its entries and reports.
  • Reports from past shifts can be re-opened, copied, or re-printed.
  • Orphaned shifts (started but abandoned with zero entries) are automatically cleaned up.
