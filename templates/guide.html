🏠 Section 1

The Homepage

The homepage is where everything starts. When you first open the app, this is the screen you land on. It shows the current date and time, and gives you a quick way to get where you need to go depending on whether you are a technician or an MST.

🕐 Live Clock and Date

Right at the top you will see today's date and a live clock. It updates on its own every minute so you always know exactly what time it is without having to look away from the screen. If a shift is already running, you will also see the shift type and the MST's name displayed right there.

📋 What Can You Do From Here?

If you are a…What to do
👷 TechnicianThe Technician View button appears on the homepage once an MST has started a shift. Click it to go to your dashboard. If no shift is active yet, the button will not be there — wait for your MST to open the shift first.
🎯 MST / LeadFill in the Start Shift form, select your shift type, enter your associate number as your PIN, and click Start. Then head to the MST Dashboard.
🔒 Techs cannot log in until the MST starts the shift. The Technician View button only appears on the homepage after a shift has been opened. If a tech says they cannot get in, the first thing to check is whether the shift has actually been started.

📅 Starting a Shift (MST Only)

The start shift form on the homepage has two things to fill in:

1
Shift Type — Select your shift from the dropdown: S1, S2, S3, S4, or S5. Pick the one that matches your schedule.
2
Your PIN — Your PIN is your Walmart associate number. Enter it here and click Start Shift. You will be taken straight to the MST Dashboard.
💡 Two shifts can run at the same time during overlap periods. For example, if S4 is finishing up while S5 is starting, both can be active simultaneously. Each shift stays open for up to one hour past its scheduled end time before the system automatically closes it. So there is no need to rush — you have time to wrap things up properly.

🔗 Other Pages You Can Reach From Here

  • History — view every past closed shift and read their reports
  • Analytics — charts and trends built from shift data over time
  • Guide — this page right here
👷 Section 2

Technician Dashboard

The Technician Dashboard is where you log the work you did during your shift. It keeps things simple — you only see your own entries, and everything you submit shows up on the MST's screen in real time so they always know what is going on.

🔐 How to Log In

⏳ You cannot log in until the MST has started the shift. The Technician View button will not appear on the homepage and the tech login page will redirect you away if no shift is running. If you cannot get in, go find your MST and let them know the shift needs to be opened first.
1
Once a shift is active, go to the homepage and click Technician View.
2
Type in your Walmart associate number and click Sign In. That is all you need — no password, no separate PIN.
3
You are in. Your sign-in time is recorded automatically and your MST can see it on their end right away.
⚠️ If your associate number is not recognized, your MST needs to add you to the roster or verify you are in the system. Head to your MST and ask them to check the Admin panel. Do not try to guess a number — just go find your MST.

📺 What You See After Signing In

Once you are in, the blue header bar at the top shows your name highlighted in gold, the current shift type, the date, and your MST's name. You will also see what time you signed on so you have a record of it.

🔴 Red OOS Banner: If any equipment is currently Out of Service, a big red warning banner will appear at the very top of your screen listing those units. Do not operate any unit on that list. They are tagged out for a reason.

📄 Uploading Your Cetaris Timecard

The main way to log your shift work is by uploading your Cetaris timecard. If you recorded your work orders in Cetaris during the shift, just export your timecard and upload it here. The system reads the file and automatically creates entries from your work orders so you do not have to type anything.

1
Export your timecard from Cetaris as a CSV or Excel file.
2
On the tech dashboard, find the Upload Cetaris Timecard section.
3
Click Choose File, select your file, and click Upload.
4
The system parses your file and builds entries from your work orders. You will see a summary of everything that was imported right away.
💡 Multiple techs can upload timecards for the same work order. The system is smart enough to automatically merge all of their notes into one clean entry in the passdown report. No duplicates, no overlap.

📋 Viewing Your Entries

At the bottom of the dashboard you will see everything you have logged this shift under My Entries. You can edit or delete your own entries if you made a mistake. Each entry shows the asset, the work order, and the time it was submitted.

✅ Signing Off

When you are finished for the shift, hit the Sign Off button. This lets your MST know you have wrapped up and are done for the day. Your entries stay on the system — signing off just marks you as complete.

🌙 Dark Mode

See the moon icon in the top right corner? Click it to flip between light and dark mode. Just a personal preference — it does not affect anything else on the system.

🎯 Section 3

MST Dashboard

The MST Dashboard is your command center for the shift. Everything happens here — you can watch tech entries come in live, manage equipment status, handle LOTO, run the PM list, and send the end-of-shift passdown email. Read through each section and you will know exactly what to do.

🔐 Logging In as MST

1
Make sure a shift has been started from the Homepage first. There needs to be an active shift before the MST dashboard will let you in.
2
Go to /lead or click MST Dashboard from the homepage.
3
Enter your Walmart associate number as your PIN and click Unlock. Your session stays active as long as the browser tab stays open.
🔒 Your associate number is your PIN. Every MST has their own. Keep it to yourself. There is a manager bypass available for emergencies — ask your manager directly if you ever need it.

📊 The Stats Bar

Pinned just below the header, the stats bar gives you a live snapshot of how the shift is going at a glance. It updates on its own so you do not need to refresh.

What You SeeWhat It Means
📋 EntriesTotal work entries logged this shift across all techs.
🔧 PMs CompletedHow many Preventive Maintenance work orders have been finished this shift.
🔴 DownAssets flagged as fully down right now.
🟡 DegradedAssets running but with a known issue being watched.
👷 Techs ActiveHow many techs have submitted at least one entry this shift.
✅ Signed OffHow many techs have clicked their Sign Off button.
⏱ Shift TimerA live clock counting up from the moment this shift was opened.

📡 The Activity Log (Live Feed)

The left side of your dashboard is the Activity Log. Every entry submitted by every tech shows up here in real time. The list refreshes automatically every 10 seconds so you always have the latest picture without doing anything. You can also filter entries by typing an asset ID or tech name into the search box at the top of the feed.

What You Can Do With Each Entry

ActionWhat It Does
🚫 ExcludePulls this entry out of the passdown report without deleting it. The entry stays in the log for records but is grayed out and will not appear in the email. Use this for anything that should not go out.
🔁 Carry OverMarks this entry to carry forward into the next shift's dashboard so the incoming MST sees it right away. Use this for anything still open or unresolved.

🔁 Carry-Overs From the Previous Shift

At the top of the Activity Log you will see anything the previous MST flagged as a carry-over. These are unresolved items handed off to you. You can Adopt them to pull them into your shift, or Dismiss them if they have already been taken care of.

🗂️ The Right Sidebar Panels

The right side of the dashboard has a stack of collapsible panels. Click any panel header to open it. Here is what each one does.

📈 Problem Assets

This panel refreshes every 30 seconds and shows which assets have been mentioned the most this shift. It ranks them by entry count so you can quickly spot anything that is getting repeated attention and might need escalation or a closer look.

📄 Cetaris CMMS Import

As the MST, you can upload a Cetaris export directly from your dashboard. This is useful if a tech forgot to upload their timecard or if you are pulling in data yourself.

1
Export the relevant work orders from Cetaris as a CSV or Excel (.xlsx) file.
2
Open the Cetaris CMMS Import panel in the right sidebar.
3
Click Choose File, select your export, and click Upload.
4
The system reads every work order row and creates or updates entries automatically. You get a summary of exactly what came in.
💡 If the same work order was touched by multiple techs and they each uploaded their own timecard, the system automatically merges all of their notes into one single entry in the passdown. The report never has duplicate lines for the same job.

🔧 Daily PMs Needing Completion

This panel shows the list of Preventive Maintenance work orders that are due and need to be completed as soon as possible. Upload the open PM report from Cetaris here as a PDF, Excel, or CSV file and the system will parse it and display each PM line by line so you can track which ones get knocked out during the shift.

Uploaded files appear in a list below the upload button. You can download a file again if you need it. If you delete a file, both the file and all the PM entries extracted from it are removed so the list stays accurate.

🔐 LOTO Key Verification

LOTO stands for Lock Out / Tag Out. At the start of every shift, you need to account for all LOTO keys to confirm that no equipment is accidentally energized while someone is working on it.

1
Open the LOTO Key Verification panel.
2
Check the All LOTO keys accounted for checkbox to confirm all keys are present.
3
If any keys are missing, enter the key number or name in the missing keys field. Missing keys show up in a list and you can remove them one by one as they turn up.
4
Click Save LOTO Status. The badge on the panel turns green when everything is verified, and stays amber while anything is still pending.
✅ LOTO status is automatically included in the passdown email so the incoming MST knows exactly what was accounted for.

🚫 Out of Service

This panel tracks every piece of equipment that is currently tagged out and unavailable for use. The moment you add something here, it shows up as a red warning banner at the top of every technician's dashboard so the whole team knows not to touch it.

1
Open the Out of Service panel.
2
Type the asset ID into the box and click Add OOS. It goes live on everyone's screen instantly.
3
When the unit is fixed and cleared, find it in the OOS list and click RTS to mark it as returned to service.

⚡ Generate Report and Send the Passdown Email

This is the most important part of the whole system. Here is exactly how the passdown email works, step by step.

Step 1 — Click Generate Report

When you are ready to wrap up, click the blue Generate Report button at the bottom of the right sidebar. The system pulls together every entry, the OOS list, LOTO status, PM count, RTS events, and any extra notes, then builds a clean formatted passdown report.

Step 2 — Click Compose Email

Once the report is generated, click the Compose Email button. This creates a fully formatted email with the complete passdown report inside and downloads it to your computer as a file ready to open in Outlook.

💡 An .eml file is basically a pre-written email sitting in your Downloads folder. Think of it like a letter that is already addressed, written, and sealed. All you have to do is open it and hit send.

Step 3 — Open the File in Outlook and Send

1
Go to your Downloads folder and look for the file named something like passdown-S5-....eml.
2
Double-click the file. It will open straight in Microsoft Outlook as a compose window.
3
The email is already filled in for you — the To address, the subject line (example: S5 Passdown), and the full report body are all there. Your Outlook signature is added at the bottom automatically.
4
Look it over one last time and click Send. Done.
✅ The passdown goes straight to the management distribution list. You do not need to type any addresses or copy and paste anything.
⚠️ Always send the email before you end the shift. The End Shift button and the email are two separate steps. Send first, then end.

What Is Inside the Email

SectionWhat It Contains
📋 HappeningsEvery work entry from the shift written out clearly. If multiple techs worked the same job, their notes are merged into one paragraph automatically.
🔐 LOTO StatusWhether all keys were verified and any missing keys listed by name.
🚫 Out of ServiceFull list of units still tagged out when the shift ended.
🔧 PMs CompletedHow many PMs were finished this shift.
✅ RTS UnitsAny equipment that was returned to service during the shift.
✍️ Your SignatureYour name, title, contact info, and the Walmart logo from your Outlook signature.

🔒 Ending the Shift

After the passdown email is sent, click the red End Shift button. This locks everything down. No more entries can be added, and the shift moves to the History tab where it is stored permanently for future reference.

⚠️ Ending the shift cannot be undone. Make absolutely sure the email is sent and everything is correct before you click End Shift.

☰ The Menu Button

The three-line menu icon in the top left corner opens a navigation menu. From there you can get to Shift History, Analytics, the Admin panel, this Guide, and the Sign Out button.

📌 Quick Reference

Common Questions

What do I use to log in?

Everyone uses their Walmart associate number. Technicians enter it at the tech login screen. MSTs enter it as their PIN on the MST dashboard. No separate passwords, no guessing — just your associate number.

I cannot see the Technician View button on the homepage.

That means no shift has been started yet. The button only appears once an MST has opened a shift. Go find your MST and let them know they need to start it from the homepage before anyone can log in.

My associate number is not being accepted at the tech login.

Go find your MST and let them know. They can check the Admin panel to make sure you are on the roster or add you if you are missing. Do not try different numbers — just go straight to your MST.

I uploaded the wrong Cetaris file.

Just upload the correct one. The system will update the entries for those work orders rather than creating duplicates. If you need an entry removed entirely, your MST can exclude it from the Activity Log.

There is an entry in the report I do not want included.

The MST can click the Exclude button on any entry in the Activity Log. It gets pulled from the report but stays in the log for records.

The .eml file will not open in Outlook.

Make sure Microsoft Outlook is installed and set as your default email program. Try right-clicking the file and choosing Open with > Microsoft Outlook. If Outlook is not installed, contact IT.

Can I generate the report more than once?

Absolutely. Click Generate Report as many times as you like. Each time it rebuilds from the latest data. Only the version you actually email out is what counts.

Where do I find old passdown reports?

Click History from the homepage or from the hamburger menu on the MST dashboard. Every closed shift is listed there with its full report ready to download.

I deleted a PM file but the PM list did not clear.

This has been fixed. Deleting a PM file now automatically removes all the PM entries that were extracted from it so the list stays accurate.
