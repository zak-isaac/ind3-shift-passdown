🏠 Section 1

The Homepage

The homepage is the starting point for everyone. When you open the app, this is the first screen you see. It shows the current date and time, lets MSTs start a new shift, and gives technicians a quick link to their own dashboard.

🕐 Live Clock & Date

At the top of the page you will see a live clock showing today's date and the current time. This updates automatically — you do not need to refresh the page. If there is already an active shift running, you will also see the shift type and MST name displayed here.

📋 What Can You Do From the Homepage?

If you are a…What to do
👷 TechnicianClick the "Tech Login" button to go to your dashboard and log your shift work.
🎯 MST / LeadFill in the Start Shift form — select shift type, enter your PIN, and click Start. Then head to the MST Dashboard.

📅 Starting a Shift (MST Only)

The start shift form on the homepage has two fields:

1
Shift Type — Select your shift from the dropdown: S1, S2, S3, S4, or S5. Pick the one that matches your actual shift schedule.
2
MST PIN — Enter your personal 4-digit PIN. Each MST has their own PIN. Do not share your PIN with anyone.
3
Click "Start Shift". You will be taken directly to the MST Dashboard where you can monitor the shift in real time.
💡 Only one shift can be active at a time. If you see a message saying a shift is already running, the previous MST needs to close their shift first — or contact the manager to end it.

🔗 Navigation Links

From the homepage you can also access:

  • History — view all past closed shifts and their reports
  • Analytics — trends and charts across multiple shifts
  • Guide — this page you are reading right now
👷 Section 2

Technician Dashboard

The Technician Dashboard is where you log everything you worked on during your shift. It is designed to be simple — you only see your own work, and everything you enter shows up on the MST's screen in real time.

🔐 How to Log In (Technicians)

1
From the homepage, click "Tech Login" or go to /tech on the app.
2
Start typing your name in the search box. The system will search the associate roster and show matching names. Click your name to select it.
3
Click "Sign In". You are now logged into the tech dashboard for this shift. Your sign-in time is recorded automatically.
💡 You do not need a password. Just find your name and sign in. If your name does not appear, contact your MST — you may need to be added to the roster.

📺 What You See After Logging In

Once you are signed in, the header bar at the top shows:

  • Your name highlighted in yellow/gold
  • The current shift type (S1, S2, etc.) and date
  • Your MST's name
  • The time you signed on
  • Your facility code (US.08972 for IND3)
🔴 OOS Banner: If any equipment is currently Out of Service, you will see a red warning banner at the top listing those units. Do NOT operate any unit shown on this list.

✏️ Logging a Manual Entry

Use this when you respond to a call, fix something, or do anything worth noting during your shift. Fill in each field and hit submit.

FieldWhat to enter
🔍 Asset / UnitThe asset ID of the equipment you worked on — e.g. M2785, BALER-01, TK51. Start typing and matching assets will appear.
📝 IssueDescribe what was wrong or why you were called. Keep it short and clear — e.g. "Motor tripping thermal breaker".
🔧 Action TakenWhat did you actually do? — e.g. "Replaced run capacitor".
✅ ResultHow did it end? — e.g. "Unit returned to service" or "Parts ordered, unit tagged OOS".
🚦 PrioritySelect the urgency level (see below).

🟠 High

🟢 Low

📄 Uploading Your Cetaris Timecard

If you log your work in Cetaris (our CMMS system), you can upload your timecard directly instead of typing each entry by hand. The system reads the file and automatically creates entries from your work orders.

1
Export your timecard from Cetaris as a CSV or Excel file.
2
On the tech dashboard, find the "Upload Cetaris Timecard" section.
3
Click "Choose File", select your file, then click "Upload".
4
The system parses your file and creates one entry per work order. You will see a confirmation with how many entries were added.
💡 Multiple techs can upload timecards for the same work order. The system is smart enough to automatically merge all their notes into one clean entry in the report — no duplicates.

📋 Viewing Your Entries

At the bottom of the tech dashboard, the "My Entries" section shows everything you have logged this shift. You can edit or delete your own entries if you made a mistake. Each entry shows the asset ID, issue, and the time it was logged.

✅ Signing Off

When you are done for the shift, click the "Sign Off" button. This tells the MST that you have finished and checked out. Your entries stay on the system — signing off just marks you as complete for the shift.

🌙 Dark Mode

There is a moon icon (🌙) in the top-right corner. Click it to switch between light and dark mode. This is just a display preference and does not affect anything else.

🎯 Section 3

MST Dashboard

The MST Dashboard is the command center for the shift lead. From here you can see every tech's entries in real time, manage equipment status, verify LOTO keys, upload reports, and send the end-of-shift passdown email. This section covers every part of it.

🔐 How to Log In (MST)

1
Make sure a shift has been started from the Homepage first (see Section 1). There must be an active shift for you to access the MST dashboard.
2
Go to /lead on the app, or click "MST Dashboard" from the homepage.
3
Enter your 4-digit MST PIN and click "Unlock". Your session stays open as long as the browser tab is open.
🔒 PINs are personal. Every MST has their own PIN. Do not share yours. Manager bypass is available for emergencies — ask your manager directly.

📊 The Stats Bar

Pinned just below the header, the stats bar gives you a live snapshot of the shift at a glance:

StatWhat it means
📋 EntriesTotal number of work entries logged this shift across all techs.
🔧 PMs CompletedHow many Preventive Maintenance work orders have been marked done this shift.
🔴 DownNumber of assets currently flagged as fully down (Critical priority).
🟡 DegradedAssets running but with a known issue being monitored.
👷 Techs ActiveHow many technicians have logged in and submitted at least one entry.
✅ Signed OffHow many techs have clicked their Sign Off button for the shift.
⏱ Shift TimerA live clock counting up from the moment this shift was started.

📡 Activity Log (Live Feed)

The left side of the MST dashboard is the Activity Log — a live, auto-refreshing list of every entry submitted by techs this shift. New entries appear automatically every 10 seconds without you needing to refresh the page.

You can filter entries by typing an asset ID or tech name into the search box in the top-right of the feed.

Actions You Can Take on Each Entry

ButtonWhat it does
✏️ EditOpens an inline edit form so you can correct the unit, issue, or priority on any entry.
🚫 ExcludeRemoves this entry from the passdown report without deleting it. The entry stays in the log for records but is greyed out and not included in the email. Use this for test entries or duplicates.
🔁 Carry OverMarks this entry to carry over into the next shift's dashboard — for ongoing issues that are not resolved yet.

🔁 Carry-Overs From Previous Shift

At the top of the activity log, you will see any entries that the previous MST flagged as "carry over." These are unresolved issues handed to you from the last shift. You can Adopt them (add them to your shift) or Dismiss them if they have since been resolved.

🗂️ Right Sidebar — All the Controls

The right side of the MST dashboard has collapsible panels for every major function. Click any panel header to open it.

📈 Problem Assets

Auto-refreshes every 30 seconds. Shows which assets have the most activity this shift — ranked by number of entries. Use this to spot repeat offenders that might need escalation.

📄 Cetaris CMMS Import (MST Upload)

As the MST, you can also upload a Cetaris export directly — without going through a tech's dashboard. This is useful if a tech forgot to upload or if you are importing shift data from Cetaris yourself.

1
Export the relevant work orders from Cetaris as a CSV or Excel (.xlsx) file.
2
Open the "Cetaris CMMS Import" panel in the right sidebar.
3
Click "Choose File", select your export, then click "Upload".
4
The system parses every work order row and creates or updates entries automatically. You will see a summary of what was imported.
💡 If the same work order was worked by multiple techs and they each uploaded their own timecard, the system automatically merges their notes into one single passdown entry — so the report never has duplicate lines for the same job.

🔧 PM's Needing Completion

This panel is for uploading the open PM (Preventive Maintenance) report from Cetaris. Upload it as a PDF, Excel, or CSV file. The system parses it and displays a list of PMs that are currently open and need to be completed. This helps track which PMs were finished during the shift.

Previously uploaded PM files are listed below the upload button. You can download them again or delete them if they are no longer needed.

🔐 LOTO Key Verification

LOTO stands for Lock Out / Tag Out. At the start of every shift, you must account for all LOTO keys to make sure no equipment is accidentally energized.

1
Open the "LOTO Key Verification" panel.
2
Check the "All LOTO keys accounted for" checkbox to confirm all keys are present.
3
If any keys are missing, type the key number/name into the missing keys field and add them. Missing keys are listed and can be removed one by one as they are located.
4
Click "Save LOTO Status". The panel badge turns green when verified, amber when pending.
✅ LOTO status is automatically included in the passdown report and email — the receiving MST will see exactly which keys were accounted for.

🚫 Out of Service (OOS)

The Out of Service panel tracks which pieces of equipment are currently tagged out and not available for use. This list is shown to every technician as a red banner at the top of their dashboard — so the whole team knows what not to operate.

1
Open the "Out of Service" panel.
2
Type the Asset ID into the input box (e.g. M2785) and click "+ Add OOS".
3
The asset immediately appears on every tech's screen in the red warning banner.
4
When the unit is repaired and back in service, find it in the OOS list and click "RTS" (Return to Service) to remove it.

📁 Happenings File Upload

This is an optional panel for uploading any supporting documents you want attached to the shift record — photos, additional reports, etc. These are stored with the shift for future reference but are not included in the passdown email automatically.

⚡ Generate Report & Send Email

This is the big one — how the passdown email actually works. Read this carefully.

Step 1 — Click "Generate Report"

At the bottom of the right sidebar, click the blue "⚡ Generate Report" button. The system pulls together every non-excluded entry, the OOS list, LOTO status, PM count, RTS events, and any extra notes you added — and builds a clean, formatted passdown report.

The report appears in the main area on the left. You can read through it and make sure everything looks right. If you need to fix something, go back to the activity log, edit or exclude entries, then click Generate Report again — it updates every time.

Step 2 — Click "Compose Email"

Once the report looks good, click the "Compose Email" button inside the report card. The system does two things at once:

  • It builds a fully-formatted HTML email with the passdown report inside.
  • It downloads a file called passdown-S5-[id].eml to your computer.
💡 An .eml file is a ready-made email file. Think of it like a pre-written letter in an envelope — it just needs you to press send.

Step 3 — Open the .eml File in Outlook

1
Go to your Downloads folder (or wherever your browser saves files).
2
Double-click the file named passdown-S5-....eml. It will open directly in Microsoft Outlook.
3
Outlook opens a compose window with the passdown already written inside — To address, subject line ("S5 Passdown"), and full report body are all pre-filled. Your own Outlook signature is added automatically at the bottom.
4
Review the email one final time. If everything looks good, click "Send".
✅ That is it. The passdown email is sent to the management distribution list automatically. You do not need to type any addresses or copy-paste anything.
⚠️ Do not close the shift before sending the email. The "End Shift" button is separate from the email. Always click Compose Email → open → send first, then end the shift.

🔒 Ending the Shift

After the passdown email has been sent, click the red "End Shift" button. This locks the shift — no more entries can be added or edited. The shift moves to the History tab where it is stored permanently.

⚠️ Ending the shift is permanent. Make sure the email is sent and everything is correct before clicking End Shift. If something was missed, check with your manager.

📌 Quick Reference

Common Questions

What is the tech PIN / password?

Technicians do not need a PIN or password. Just search for your name and click Sign In.

I can't find my name in the tech login search.

Your name might not be in the associate roster yet. Let your MST know and they can add you through the Admin panel, or reload the roster from there.

I uploaded the wrong Cetaris file — what do I do?

Upload the correct file again. The system will update the existing entries for the same work orders rather than creating duplicates. If you need to remove an entry entirely, your MST can exclude it from the activity log.

The report shows an entry I don't want included.

The MST can click the 🚫 Exclude button on any entry in the activity log. Excluded entries are greyed out and not included in the generated report — but they stay in the log for records.

The shift timer shows --:-- on the MST dashboard.

Refresh the page. The timer loads automatically when the stats bar appears — if the bar took a moment to load, the timer should catch up within a second.

The .eml file won't open in Outlook.

Make sure Microsoft Outlook is installed and set as your default email client. Right-click the .eml file → "Open with" → Microsoft Outlook. If Outlook is not installed, contact IT.

Can I generate the report more than once?

Yes! Click Generate Report as many times as you need. Each time it rebuilds from the latest entry data. Only the final version you email is what counts.

Where do I see old passdown reports?

Click History from the homepage or hamburger menu. Every closed shift is listed there with its full report and the ability to download it.
